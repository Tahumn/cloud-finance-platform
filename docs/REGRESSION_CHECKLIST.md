# Regression checklist

Updated: 2026-07-29

## 1. Auth
- [ ] Dang ky tai khoan moi thanh cong.
- [ ] Dang nhap bang email hoac username thanh cong.
- [ ] Dang xuat xoa session va quay ve man hinh auth.

## 2. Onboarding
- [ ] Tao tai khoan/vi ban dau thanh cong.
- [ ] Nhap so du ban dau va han muc the tin dung luu dung.

## 3. Accounts
- [ ] Tao tai khoan moi thanh cong.
- [ ] Sua tai khoan cap nhat dung so du, han muc, provider, last4.
- [ ] Lich su bien dong tai khoan hien thi sau khi tao/sua.
- [ ] Loc theo loai, sap xep, va an tai khoan so du 0 hoat dong dung.

## 4. Transactions
- [ ] Danh sach giao dich tai day du, khong mat trang sau.
- [ ] Loc theo ngay, loai, danh muc, tai khoan, keyword hoat dong dung.
- [ ] Tao giao dich thu/chi/chuyen khoan thanh cong.
- [ ] Sua giao dich cap nhat dung so tien va tag.
- [ ] Xoa giao dich hoan tra du lieu lien quan dung.

## 5. Tags va Categories
- [ ] Tao tag thanh cong.
- [ ] Sua tag thanh cong.
- [ ] Xoa tag thanh cong va khong con xuat hien trong form giao dich.
- [ ] Tao/sua/xoa category khong lam vo bo loc.

## 6. Budgets
- [ ] Tao budget thanh cong.
- [ ] Sua, pause/resume, complete, delete budget hoat dong dung.
- [ ] Canh bao vuot nguong hien thi dung khi co giao dich phat sinh.

## 7. Bills
- [ ] Tao bill thu cong thanh cong.
- [ ] Sua trang thai bill (pending/paid/overdue) cap nhat dung.
- [ ] Tim kiem bill theo ten/ma hoa don hoat dong dung.

## 8. Goals / Savings
- [ ] Tao goal thanh cong.
- [ ] Dong gop nhanh vao goal luu vao backend.
- [ ] Lich su dong gop hien thi dung theo tung goal.

## 9. OCR
- [ ] Tai file OCR va trich xuat duoc thong tin co ban.
- [ ] Chinh sua thong tin OCR truoc khi luu giao dich.
- [ ] Tao giao dich tu OCR thanh cong.

## 10. Chat AI
- [ ] Tai lich su chat tu backend neu co.
- [ ] Gui cau hoi va nhan phan hoi AI thanh cong.
- [ ] Neu AI loi, UI hien thong bao loi ro rang va khong chen mock reply.

## 11. Settings / Profile
- [ ] Sua ho so cap nhat lai thong tin user tren UI ngay sau khi luu.
- [ ] Doi mat khau goi dung backend va reset form sau khi thanh cong.
- [ ] Toggle thong bao luu ca local state va backend field tuong ung.
- [ ] Doi ngon ngu, theme, layout cap nhat UI ngay lap tuc.

## 12. Reports / Dashboard
- [ ] Dashboard khong vo khi khong co du lieu.
- [ ] Reports van hien du lieu khi tap giao dich nho.
- [ ] So lieu tong hop khop voi danh sach giao dich da loc.
